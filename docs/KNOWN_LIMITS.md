# Belto beta: what to expect

Thank you for testing Belto. It's an early version, so here is what is and isn't solid yet. Telling us what you find is the whole point.

## How to get the best experience

- **Use a real browser**: Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android, or Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a laptop. Links opened inside Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and similar apps usually can't use the microphone. Belto will tell you to open the page in your browser.
- **Allow the microphone** when asked. Your voice is analysed on your device. It is never recorded or uploaded.
- **Wired headphones or the phone's own speaker work best.** Bluetooth headphones add delay and often switch the mic to low quality, which makes rhythm and harmony scores unfair.
- **A quiet room helps.** Fans and traffic can confuse the pitch detector.
- **On an iPhone**, if you can't hear the demo notes, check the silent switch on the side.

## Things that are known to be rough

- Scores are **estimates** and depend on your microphone and room. A bad score is not a verdict on your voice.
- **The leaderboard is on the honour system.** Progress is reported by each person's own app, so it can be gamed. Please don't.
- **Some skills can't be graded by a microphone**: clear words, a "bright" tone, posture, and whether a belt is safe. Those lessons use checklists and tell you so.
- **Progress lives on your device first.** If you clear your browser data without signing in, it's gone. Sign in to back it up. Use **Profile > Download my data** any time.
- **Bluetooth, hearing-aid streaming and some USB mics** can behave oddly. Tell us what you used.
- Belto needs an internet connection to open. It doesn't work offline yet.

## Looking after your voice

Singing should never hurt. Stop if anything feels scratchy, tight or sore, rest, and drink water. The Power & belting lessons are the ones to be careful with: strong is not the same as strained.

## Your data

- You can **download everything we hold** and **delete your account** from the Profile page.
- You can **block or report** anyone from the Friends page.
- Details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms linked from the app.

## Tell us

Use **Profile > Tell us** (it fills in your device details), or open an issue with the Bug report or Feedback form. Useful things to include: which lesson, what you did, what you expected, what happened, and what you were singing into.
